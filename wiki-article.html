Getting started

How to begin your life in Putrajaya.

Putrajaya of Roblox is built around roleplay, rules, and meaningful community interaction. Every citizen has a place in the city, whether you serve the public, lead a business, support the community, or simply explore the streets and meet new people.

“A successful city is built by the people who show up, contribute, and help one another.”

1. Join the community

Start by learning the local culture, meeting the community, and reading the rules. New players are encouraged to observe the city first before choosing a role.

2. Pick a role

Roles can include public service, business ownership, law or government service, transportation, hospitality, or everyday citizen life. Choose a role that fits your interests and contributes to the city.

3. Learn the culture

Successful roleplay depends on respect, cooperation, and consistency. Follow the city’s standards, communicate clearly, and support other players.

4. Stay involved

Keep an eye on the main community updates, announcements, and in-game events. Participation creates stronger stories and a more active city.

5. Build your story

Your experience is shaped by the choices you make. Make your role memorable, contribute to the city, and help create a positive environment for everyone.
