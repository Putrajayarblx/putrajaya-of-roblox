Community news

Welcome to the new Putrajaya of Roblox.

Putrajaya of Roblox is continuing to grow — and with each new story, we are creating a stronger roleplay environment for residents, visitors, and community leaders alike.

The city is designed around service, character, and belonging. Our goal is to make Putrajaya feel like a place with purpose: a place where players can form identities, build trust, and create experiences that last beyond a single session.

“Whether you are serving the public, building a business, or welcoming new citizens, every role matters.”

What changes next?

We are expanding the city’s structure, creating clearer pathways for new citizens, and preparing stronger systems for community engagement. As the city evolves, we will improve communication, add more opportunities, and support a more immersive environment.

How you can help

Take part in the community, contribute to the stories around you, and help welcome others into the world we are building together. Every resident adds a little more life to the city.
